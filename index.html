The body

NV-01

A 1.25-meter biped. About 34 kilograms. 23 driven joints. No hands. No neck. A CRT-monitor head with two vertical pill eyes — or no head at all, depending on which photo you are looking at.

Shanghai RoboParty published the CAD. On this bench it is NV-01. Same baseline. We are pointing this serial at humanoid combat.

1.25 mHeight. ATOM 01 manual.
33.76 kgURDF V2 inertials. Press says 34.
23 DOF12 legs, 1 waist, 10 arms. Not 20.
~40 kgFight class. Not an 80 kg T800.
120 N·mLeg / waist class. DM 10010L.
27 N·mArm / ankle class. DM 4340P.
48 V15 Ah pack on the lower back.
55 STLShell panels. Static display until we revalidate impact.

The job

Not on a card yet

Norevert is not on a published URKL, CyberHero, REK, or UFB card. The work is the body and the policies. House rules until a league writes us in: three rounds, two minutes, thirty seconds rest. Tether until get-up holds on hardware.

The walking GIFs still have a person on the back handle. Both facts stay true. We do not write 3 m/s. We have not timed it.

Open

Files, not a press render

Most open humanoids give you a URDF and a render. The baseline gives you a full SolidWorks assembly, production Gerbers, and a versioned parts list. That is why this body is worth turning into a fighter.
