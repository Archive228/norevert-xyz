01 — The body

What you are
actually looking at

A 1.25-meter biped. About 34 kilograms. 23 driven joints. No hands. No neck. A display-box head with two vertical pill eyes, or no head at all, depending on which photo you are looking at.

The serial is NV-01. The chest mark is Norevert. We are pointing this body at the 40-kilogram cage.

02 — Inside

23 joints,
two motors

Nine DM 10010L carry the weight: three per hip, one per knee, one in the waist. Fourteen DM 4340P do everything else: five per arm, two per ankle. The ankle is the signature. Two motors sit high on the shin and drive the foot through two silver rods. If someone sends you an “is this the same robot?” photo, look at the ankle before the face.

Four CAN buses leave the RBE board: left leg, right leg plus waist, left arm, right arm. Yellow labels on the real looms exist for a reason.

05 — Lab, not a mascot

What actually
walks

CNC frame. X-truss chest. Blue pack in the cutout. Yellow tape on the looms. No head. Hoist ropes from the upper corners. Every honest walk we have starts with ropes or a hand on the rear handle. The handle is a designed part. We treat it as part of the robot.

06 — The job

Not on a card yet

Norevert is not on a published URKL, CyberHero, REK or UFB card. If a sentence needs that slot to work, we cut the sentence. The work is the body and the policies: retarget onto this skeleton, train get-up first, guard second. A jab that dumps the robot is not a jab.

Legs are a balance policy. Arms are a pad or a headset. Unlocking all 23 joints in teleop without a balance policy is a faceplant, not a combo.

House rules until a league writes us in

  1. Three rounds, two minutes each.
  2. Thirty seconds rest between rounds.
  3. Tether on until get-up holds on hardware.
  4. First hits are a bag on a gantry. Not a person. Not an untethered spar.
  5. A glove is a foam cup on the flange. EVA on chest and shoulders, tape on the shins.
  6. Every published walk says whether a hand is on the bar. Every strike says what it hit.

Things we do not write

  • 20 degrees of freedom. The URDF is 23.
  • 3 m/s. Nobody timed it. We have not measured a meter.
  • Kicked it and it stood up. That clip sits next to a Unitree G1.
  • A flying kick that takes a head off. That was a T800-class machine on someone else’s night.
  • Stairs as a first-party demo. We have a third-party title, not a checked tape.
  • A G1 boxing brain. 29 actions on different kinematics. We train on this skeleton.
  • A slot on a league card. Building for the cage is not a slot.
