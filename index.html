The body

NV-01

A 1.25-meter biped. About 34 kilograms. 23 driven joints. No hands. No neck. The walking GIFs still have a person on the back handle. Both facts stay true.

Shanghai RoboParty published the CAD. Notices stay. On this bench the serial is NV-01. The chest mark is Norevert — not their wordmark.

What actually walks

Lab, not a mascot

CNC frame. X-truss chest. Blue pack in the cutout. Yellow tape on the looms. No head. Hoist ropes from the upper corners. This is the honest fight look.

The job

Not on a card yet

Norevert is not on a published URKL, CyberHero, REK, or UFB card. The work is the body and the policies. House rules until a league writes us in: three rounds, two minutes, thirty seconds rest. Tether until get-up holds on hardware.

We do not write 3 m/s. We have not timed it. A glove is a foam cup on the flange.
