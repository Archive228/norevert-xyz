02 — Current build

One bot.
One evolving body.

NV-01 is the first robot from Norevert. Its body starts with an open humanoid platform and develops through individual hardware and software upgrades.

03 — The idea

Research.
Trade. Upgrade.

We built its first body. Now we are developing the system that can help pay for what comes next.

NV-01 researches on-chain projects, connects claims to repositories and market data, and evaluates a defined trading strategy. The target ecosystem is Robinhood Chain.

That chain is separate from the Robinhood brokerage app. This is an independent project.

A hardware goal starts with a clear purpose and a parts budget. Confirmed trading proceeds can be allocated to that goal, then connected to its purchase and installation.

People still design the mounts and install the parts. The objective is to give the software a growing physical presence, with an understandable record of how each upgrade happened.
