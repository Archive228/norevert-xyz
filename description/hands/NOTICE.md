# AmazingHand display geometry

Source: Pollen Robotics / AmazingHand, commit
`3e8241074df3436a3044ced4881e3bb2133aa725`.

https://github.com/pollen-robotics/AmazingHand/tree/3e8241074df3436a3044ced4881e3bb2133aa725

The authors license the mechanical design under **Creative Commons Attribution
4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
The original source and its license statement are available at the link above.

These derivatives combine the right and left MJCF reference assemblies and their
STLs, rotate them into the website's forearm coordinates, cluster vertices at
1 mm, and quantize positions for the viewer. Colors are derived from the source
materials. `PROVENANCE.json` records the transformations, counts and binary hash.
The website applies a uniform 2/3 display scale around each wrist origin;
the source CAD and packed geometry retain their original dimensions.

Adapted by Norevert for a translucent planned-upgrade preview. The placement on
NV-01 is conceptual: it is not an engineered mounting adapter, a fit certification,
or a claim that either hand is installed. Right hand first; left hand later.
